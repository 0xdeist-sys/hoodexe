FAQ

Built simple.

Do I need to stake?

No. Your STONEFOLK stays in your wallet.

When does claiming open?

Wallet connection is available immediately. Claiming opens 24 hours after mint begins: August 10, 2026 at 17:00 Turkey time. After activation, available rewards can be claimed without a weekly manual payout.

Is the Reward Box balance live?

Yes after the Reward Vault address is configured. The site reads the wallet balance directly from Robinhood Chain.

What happens when I sell?

The intended model resets that NFT's time-based Holding Power for the new owner. Final transfer accounting will be fixed in the deployed contract.
